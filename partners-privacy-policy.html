Privacy Policy

Effective Date: April 29, 2025
Last Updated: April 29, 2025

Welcome to SearchIn Partners. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, disclose, and protect the information of users—including order pickers, packers, delivery partners, and inventory staff—who use our app to support operations for vendor stores partnered with SearchIn.

By using the SearchIn Partners app, you agree to the practices outlined in this policy.

1. Information We Collect

a. Personal Information

  • Name
  • Phone number
  • Government-issued ID
  • Bank or UPI details

b. Location Data

  • Real-time GPS location

c. Device Information

  • Device type and operating system
  • IP address and unique device identifiers

d. Work-related Information

  • Assigned orders and tasks
  • Order fulfillment and delivery status
  • Inventory entries and updates
  • Task timestamps (e.g., check-in, picked, packed, delivered)

2. How We Use Your Information

  • Assign and track tasks
  • Communicate work updates
  • Monitor task status and performance
  • Process payments
  • Ensure operational accuracy and accountability
  • Improve app functionality and performance
  • Maintain user safety and prevent misuse

3. Information Sharing and Disclosure

We do not sell your personal information. We may share data with:

  • Vendor Stores: For task management and performance tracking
  • SearchIn Internal Teams: For operations, support, and audits
  • Payment Gateways: To process payouts
  • Government Authorities: When legally required
  • Technical Partners: Who assist with app infrastructure under strict data security agreements

4. Data Security

We use secure encryption and restricted access to protect your information. However, users are responsible for safeguarding their login credentials and device access.

5. Your Rights

  • View and update your profile information
  • Request data deletion, subject to any legal or operational requirements

6. Data Retention

We retain your data for as long as required to fulfill operational needs and comply with regulations. After inactivity or contract termination, data may be deleted or anonymized.

7. Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy from time to time. You will be notified of significant changes through the app. Continued use of the app after updates indicates your agreement with the revised policy.
